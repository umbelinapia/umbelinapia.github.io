Autonomous Cylinder Pick System

2025–2026 · Group project · Loughborough University

Outcome

One of only 3 of 13 teams in the cohort to deliver a successful system, achieving 100% pick-and-place success across three evaluation runs.

Motivation

A cohort-wide group project to build a 3-DOF gantry robot capable of autonomously detecting and picking cylinders using machine vision.

My Contribution

I designed the mechanical structure and the machine-vision pipeline — using Gaussian blur and Hough circle detection — for cylinder detection.

Skills & Learning

Machine Vision Hough Circle Detection Gaussian Blur SolidWorks (CAD) Python Team Collaboration OpenCV ESP32-S3 Stepper Motor Control Limit-Switch Homing Camera-to-Robot Calibration Safety Interlocks Serial Command Protocol Wi-Fi Camera Streaming Budget Management Iterative Testing Technical Presentation

Technical Details

  • 3-DOF gantry robot configuration.
  • Mechanical structure designed in SolidWorks.
  • Machine-vision pipeline: Gaussian blur followed by Hough circle detection for cylinder localisation.
  • 100% pick-and-place success across three evaluation runs.

What I Learned

  • Turning camera pixels into robot coordinates by calibrating against the real workspace, including correcting for an angled camera view.
  • Making a robot repeatable: homing every joint against a limit switch and adding safety interlocks in the firmware.
  • Splitting a system into a laptop vision script and an ESP32 arm controller that talk through a simple serial handshake.
  • Working as a team to a tight budget, testing in small steps, and demonstrating live under time pressure.
